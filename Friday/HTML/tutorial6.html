Semantic Tags Link tags and Anchors tag

Semantic tages are the tags that clearly describe the purpose of content inside them

Why do we use them?

    They make website code easier to understand, organize, and maintain.

    They also help search engines and accessibility tools understand the page structure.

Important Sematic tag include :-
  1. header:- Top section of a page or section

  2. nav:-Navigation links

  3. main:- Main content of the page

  4. section:-Groups related content
  5. article:- Independent content
  6. footer:- Bottom section
