Accessibility & SEO

Accessibility – Making websites usable for everyone, including people with disabilities.

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) – Making websites easier for Google and other search engines to understand and show in search results.

Accessibility

Simple definition: Accessibility means designing a website in such a way that everyone can use it, including people who cannot see, hear, or use a mouse.

Imagine: You develop an online shopping website. - A normal person can see the product image. - A blind person cannot see the product image. - But a screen reader can read text that describes the image.

Where is it used? - Banking websites - Government websites - Shopping websites - Educational websites - Hospital websites - Almost every professional website

1.1 ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications) Simple definition: ARIA provides additional information to screen readers so people with disabilities can understand website elements.
